Child Safety Standards Policy

Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) & Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

Application: IKTA Smart Dialer Package Name: com.ikta.iktasmartdialer Developer: ABS Software Effective Date: November 2026

1. Zero Tolerance for CSAE & CSAM

ABS Software maintains an absolute, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) across IKTA Smart Dialer (com.ikta.iktasmartdialer) and all associated community or caller-identification features.

Any creation, uploading, sharing, solicitation, storage, or distribution of content that exploits, abuses, grooms, or endangers minors is strictly prohibited.

2. Scope & Platform Safeguards

IKTA Smart Dialer is built with a privacy-first architecture where user contacts and call histories remain strictly on the user's device. For optional community-driven features—such as crowdsourced spam reporting, caller photo sharing, and user network profiles—we enforce the following safeguards:

  • Age Requirement: IKTA Smart Dialer is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed toward children.
  • Prohibited Media & Profile Content: Users are strictly forbidden from uploading or sharing inappropriate, abusive, or exploitative caller profile images, display names, or spam reports.
  • Immediate Content Removal: Any flagged or suspected CSAM/CSAE material is removed immediately upon detection or report.

3. In-App Reporting & User Feedback Mechanism

IKTA Smart Dialer provides an accessible in-app reporting mechanism allowing users to report abusive callers, inappropriate caller photos, or any child safety concerns directly from the caller profile, network screen, or Help & Support section.

  • All child safety reports are prioritized for urgent review by our moderation team.
  • Confirmed violators have their accounts and shared profile data permanently banned and blocked from the platform.

4. Legal Compliance & Law Enforcement Reporting

ABS Software complies fully with all applicable national and international child protection laws, including India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act and Information Technology Act guidelines.

Upon becoming aware of any confirmed CSAM or CSAE activity, we preserve relevant evidence as required by law and promptly report the incident to regional and national law enforcement authorities as well as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
