Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set IKTA Smart Dialer as my default phone app?
Open IKTA Smart Dialer → Click on the banner at the top asking for Default Phone App → Select "IKTA Smart Dialer" and tap "Set as default". Alternatively, go to your phone's Android Settings → Apps → Default Apps → Phone App → Choose IKTA Smart Dialer.
How does the TRAI 1909 DND spam reporting work?
Whenever an unknown or suspected telemarketing number calls you, tap the "TRAI 1909" badge on their call card. IKTA Smart Dialer automatically formats the official SMS complaint string with the date, time, and caller number and opens your default SMS app addressed to 1909. All you need to do is press Send!
Can I use IKTA Smart Dialer completely offline?
Yes! All primary features—including T9 dialing, local contact searches, call logging, phone storage profile pictures, and caller screening—run completely on-device without requiring an internet connection.
