Step-by-Step Setup & Usage Instructions

1

Install & Set as Default Phone App

Open IKTA Smart Dialer after installation and tap "Set as Default" when prompted by Android. This allows the app to handle outgoing/incoming calls, display Caller ID, and manage your call history.

2

Grant Essential Telecommunications Permissions

Allow Phone, Contacts, and Call Logs permissions so you can search contacts quickly, view recent call durations, and manage blocked numbers.

3

Report Spam Calls via TRAI 1909

After disconnecting an unsolicited commercial call, open the call entry or post-call summary and select Report Spam (1909) to format and submit a DND complaint.

4

Play Educational Quizzes & Earn Virtual IKTA Coins

Navigate to the Win Quiz & Collect Coins section, optionally watch a rewarded video ad to unlock quiz rounds, and answer general knowledge questions to earn virtual IKTA Coins and Study Group ranking points.
