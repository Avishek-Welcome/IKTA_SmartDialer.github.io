Trust & Compliance

Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: November 2026. IKTA Smart Dialer is committed to absolute transparency, zero-data selling, and robust user data protection.

1. Core Commitment & Privacy Philosophy

IKTA Smart Dialer ("we", "our", or "the app") provides a high-performance default phone calling experience. We believe personal communications, address books, and call logs belong exclusively to the device owner.

Zero Commercial Data Selling: We do NOT sell, lease, rent, trade, or monetize your personal contacts, phone numbers, or call log entries with data brokers, advertisers, or analytics collectors.

All contact directories and call histories are saved locally inside your device's isolated SQLite Room database sandbox.

2. Android System Permissions & Purpose

As a full-fledged replacement for your system default dialer, IKTA Smart Dialer requests standard Android Telecom and Contact permissions. Below is the explicit purpose of each permission:

Android Permission Core Functionality & Exact Purpose
READ_CALL_LOG / WRITE_CALL_LOG Displaying recent incoming, outgoing, and missed communications on the Home screen; logging completed calls; recording durations; and allowing user-initiated call deletion.
READ_CONTACTS / WRITE_CONTACTS Resolving caller names from your phone book, fast T9 dialpad search, multi-account sync (Google/Device/SIM), and saving new contacts.
CALL_PHONE & BIND_INCALL_SERVICE Directly placing voice calls via your chosen SIM card and managing active in-call audio, speakerphone, hold, mute, and DTMF dialpad tones.
READ_PHONE_STATE & READ_PHONE_NUMBERS Detecting carrier subscription accounts (SIM 1 and SIM 2) and handling multi-SIM call routing.
USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT Displaying the interactive full-screen incoming call card when the phone screen is locked or idle, ensuring you never miss a call.
CAMERA Exclusively utilized on user demand during the optional 3-Blink Liveness check for setting up your personal verified caller photo. No video or background streaming occurs.

3. Caller ID & AES-256 Encrypted Data Storage

IKTA Smart Dialer features two distinct privacy configurations under the Caller ID & Profile Privacy settings:

  • Encrypted Data Mode (Default): Your phone number is encrypted via AES-256 and stored purely as an encrypted token. Your selfie, name, and identity are completely private and never broadcast to other users.
  • Visible for All (Global): When explicitly enabled by you, your compressed profile avatar (≤10 KB) is made visible to other users when placing or receiving unknown calls to deter spam and fraud.

4. Advertisements & Google UMP GDPR Consent

To support free ongoing software development, the app displays non-intrusive advertisements served through Google AdMob and certified mediation network adapters (Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, InMobi, Mintegral, Unity Ads).

We integrate the official Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to collect GDPR, UK, and Indian consent parameters before requesting personalized ads. Users may revoke or update their consent preferences at any time.

5. Data Retention, Control & Deletion

You hold absolute control over your stored data. You can delete individual or all call logs directly from the Recent Communications screen, reset photo sync caches, or uninstall the app to immediately wipe all local SQLite database records.
